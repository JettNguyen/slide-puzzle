How to play

Moving tiles

Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it. Dragging works too, and dragging a tile with others in the way slides the whole row.

On a keyboard, the arrow keys slide tiles in that direction.

Goal

Arrange the numbers 1 to 15 in order, with the gap in the bottom-right corner.
